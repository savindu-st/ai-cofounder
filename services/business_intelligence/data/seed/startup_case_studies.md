# Curated Startup Vertical Case Studies

Benchmark profiles across SaaS, Marketplace, Developer Tool, and other tech business models.

---

## Case Study 1: B2B SaaS Vertical (Linear / Notion Model)
- **Model:** Product-Led Growth (PLG) freemium with seat-based subscription tiers.
- **Key Metrics:** CAC payback period < 12 months, Net Revenue Retention (NRR) > 120%, Monthly Churn < 1.5%.
- **Channels:** High-quality engineering content, viral workspace invitations, word of mouth.
- **Cost Structure:** Cloud infrastructure (AWS/GCP), high compensation for top-tier product designers and engineers.

## Case Study 2: Two-Sided Marketplace (Airbnb / DoorDash Model)
- **Model:** Take-rate percentage on Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) typically 15%–25%.
- **Key Challenge:** Chicken-and-egg supply vs. demand constraint.
- **Solution:** Subsidize supply side first in constrained geographical micro-markets.
- **Economics:** High initial CAC amortized over repeat booking lifecycle.

## Case Study 3: AI-Augmented Workflow Automation (Modern Co-Founder Model)
- **Model:** Monthly recurring subscription with usage-based token overage tiers.
- **Critical Unit Economics:** COGS heavily influenced by inference API costs. Target gross margins: > 70% by caching repeated queries and using deterministic local compute where possible.
- **Retention Strategy:** Deep workflow integration and proprietary historical project context that increases switching costs.

## Case Study 4: Fintech Infrastructure & API (Stripe / Plaid Model)
- **Model:** Usage-based fee (percentage + fixed fee per transaction/API call).
- **Key Metrics:** Transaction volume processed (TPV), developer adoption, API reliability (99.99% uptime).
- **Channels:** World-class developer documentation, hackathons, SDKs, startup partner programs.
- **Cost Structure:** Bank partnership compliance, fraud and chargeback reserves, banking rail settlement costs.

## Case Study 5: Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) E-Commerce (Warby Parker Model)
- **Model:** Vertical integration with high gross margins (60%-70%) selling direct-to-consumer online and offline.
- **Key Metrics:** Repeat order rate, Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) > 3.0x.
- **Channels:** Meta/TikTok performance ads, influencer partnerships, pop-up experiential retail.
- **Cost Structure:** Inventory carrying costs, fulfillment and 3PL warehousing, customer returns management.

## Case Study 6: EdTech & Professional Reskilling (Coursera / Maven Model)
- **Model:** Cohort-based course fees or enterprise B2B upskilling subscriptions.
- **Key Metrics:** Course completion rates, student Net Promoter Score (NPS), employer sponsorship rate.
- **Channels:** Subject matter expert instructor audiences, LinkedIn organic content, corporate L&D partnerships.
- **Cost Structure:** Instructor rev-share (20%-50%), video production, platform community moderation.

## Case Study 7: HealthTech & Telemedicine (Hims / Ro Model)
- **Model:** Telehealth consultation fee bundled with recurring subscription for medical treatments.
- **Key Metrics:** Active subscribers, lifetime refill duration (months), regulatory adherence.
- **Channels:** Direct consumer marketing, search intent for specific conditions, medical professional referral.
- **Cost Structure:** Licensed clinical network overhead, prescription fulfillment pharmacy partnerships, HIPAA compliance.

## Case Study 8: Logistics & Freight Brokerage (Flexport Model)
- **Model:** Freight forwarding margin on freight volume moved plus software visibility subscription.
- **Key Metrics:** On-time arrival rate, customs clearance speed, container volume utilization.
- **Channels:** Enterprise logistics procurement RFPs, supply chain audit webinars.
- **Cost Structure:** Working capital for carrier advances, bonded customs agents, freight liability insurance.

## Case Study 9: PropTech & Real Estate SaaS (Procore Model)
- **Model:** High ACV ($10k - $100k+) annual subscriptions based on construction volume managed.
- **Key Metrics:** Annual Contract Value (ACV), Gross Revenue Retention (>95%), field worker mobile daily active usage.
- **Channels:** Direct enterprise field sales reps, construction trade shows, subcontractor referral networks.
- **Cost Structure:** Enterprise customer success and implementation consultants, legacy CAD/BIM integrations.

## Case Study 10: FoodTech & Cloud Kitchens (Nextbite Model)
- **Model:** Virtual brand licensing; delivery platform revenue sharing with existing underutilized restaurant kitchens.
- **Key Metrics:** Food prep time, third-party delivery marketplace ranking, customer ratings (>4.5 stars).
- **Channels:** UberEats/DoorDash promotional bidding, delivery app algorithmic ranking optimization.
- **Cost Structure:** Packaging branding, ingredient supplier agreements, delivery commission fees (20%-30%).

## Case Study 11: CleanTech & Energy Intelligence (Arcadia Model)
- **Model:** Subscription billing management fee and utility data API access for enterprise ESG compliance.
- **Key Metrics:** Renewable energy capacity contracted (MW), utility meter integration coverage.
- **Channels:** Municipal partnerships, enterprise ESG officer sales, utility company billing integrations.
- **Cost Structure:** Regulatory legal counsel, custom meter parsing infrastructure, carbon accounting audit certs.

## Case Study 12: InsurTech (Lemonade Model)
- **Model:** Underwriting premium retention with fixed fee cut (e.g., 25% flat fee) while remaining premium pool covers claims.
- **Key Metrics:** Loss ratio (< 70%), claim turnaround speed (AI claims adjudication), policyholder growth.
- **Channels:** In-app seamless onboarding, tenant/renter association partnerships, SEO for insurance quote terms.
- **Cost Structure:** Reinsurance premiums, state insurance licensing bonds, fraud detection algorithms.

## Case Study 13: Creator Economy & Monetization Platform (Substack Model)
- **Model:** 10% platform take-rate on creator subscription revenue.
- **Key Metrics:** Gross Publication Value (GPV), subscriber churn, network recommendation cross-pollination.
- **Channels:** Top-tier creator recruitment with signing bonuses, creator recommendation network loops.
- **Cost Structure:** Payment processing fees (Stripe Connect), media hosting infrastructure, editorial moderation.

## Case Study 14: Cybersecurity & DevSecOps (Snyk Model)
- **Model:** Developer-focused freemium scanning with enterprise governance and vulnerability remediation tiers.
- **Key Metrics:** Vulnerabilities remediated, GitHub/GitLab integration installs, developer conversion to paid.
- **Channels:** Open-source vulnerability database, IDE extension marketplace, CI/CD pipeline integrations.
- **Cost Structure:** Security vulnerability research lab, heavy continuous scanner server capacity, enterprise compliance certifications (SOC2, FedRAMP).

## Case Study 15: HRTech & Global Employment (Deel / Remote Model)
- **Model:** Monthly flat SaaS fee per active contractor ($35–$49/mo) and per full-time EOR employee ($500+/mo).
- **Key Metrics:** Number of active contractors/employees managed, country entity coverage, contractor retention.
- **Channels:** Startup incubator perks, remote work community content, VC portfolio company deals.
- **Cost Structure:** In-country legal entity maintenance, localized tax law compliance engines, foreign exchange handling.
