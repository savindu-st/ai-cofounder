# Ash Maurya Lean Canvas Framework Guide

The Lean Canvas is an adaptation of Alexander Osterwalder's Business Model Canvas designed specifically for startups, emphasizing problem validation and actionable early testing.

## Key Lean Canvas Elements

### 1. Problem
- Top 1–3 problems your target customers face.
- Existing alternatives: How are these problems solved today (e.g., spreadsheets, manual work, competing tools)?

### 2. Customer Segments
- Target customers: The primary audience experiencing the acute problem.
- Early adopters: The specific subset who feel the pain most acutely and actively search for a solution.

### 3. Unique Value Proposition (UVP)
- Single, clear, compelling message that states why you are different and worth paying attention to.
- High-level concept: An X for Y analogy (e.g., "Uber for dog walking", "GitHub for design").

### 4. Solution
- Top 3 features that directly solve the top 3 stated problems.

### 5. Channels
- Paths to customers (inbound and outbound).
- Focus on scalable, repeatable acquisition channels over non-repeatable hacks.

### 6. Revenue Streams
- Revenue model, life time value (LTV), pricing tiers, gross margins.

### 7. Cost Structure
- Customer acquisition costs (CAC), distribution costs, hosting, personnel.

### 8. Key Metrics
- The key activities you measure (AARRR: Acquisition, Activation, Retention, Referral, Revenue).

### 9. Unfair Advantage
- Something that cannot be easily copied or bought (e.g., insider information, proprietary algorithms, network effects, personal authority).
